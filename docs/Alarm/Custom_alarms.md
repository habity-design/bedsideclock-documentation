# Custom alarms

With a custom schedule each alarm has its own time and its own days. You can create a maximum of 4 alarms. Use it if you wake up at different times during the week, or want a second alarm on some days.

## Turn on custom schedule

Go to "Alarm" > "Settings" and press the first row until it reads "Custom schedule".

## Create an alarm

Go to "Alarm" > "Set alarm" to see your alarms.

**1. Create the alarm.** Select "Create alarm" at the bottom of the list.

<div class="hb-screens">
  <figure><img src="/screens/custom-alarms/alarm-list-one.png" alt="Alarm list with Create alarm selected"><figcaption>Select "Create alarm".</figcaption></figure>
</div>

**2. Set the time.** Turn the selector wheel to choose the hour and press. Then choose the minutes and press.

<div class="hb-screens">
  <figure><img src="/screens/custom-alarms/set-hours.png" alt="Alarm time screen with the hours underlined"><figcaption>Set the hour, then the minutes.</figcaption></figure>
</div>

**3. Choose the days.** A new alarm starts with no days. Turn to a day and press to turn it on or off. Days filled in black are on.

<div class="hb-screens">
  <figure><img src="/screens/custom-alarms/schedule-empty.png" alt="Days screen with no days selected"><figcaption>A new alarm starts with no days.</figcaption></figure>
  <figure><img src="/screens/custom-alarms/schedule-weekdays.png" alt="Days screen with Monday to Friday filled"><figcaption>Monday to Friday on.</figcaption></figure>
</div>

**4. Save.** Turn to the arrow and press to save the alarm.

Your alarms are listed in order of time. You can have up to four. With four alarms, "Create alarm" is hidden.

<div class="hb-screens">
  <figure><img src="/screens/custom-alarms/alarm-list-two.png" alt="Alarm list with alarms at 06:30 and 07:00"><figcaption>Two alarms, earliest first.</figcaption></figure>
</div>

::: tip
If you create an alarm at a time you already have, the clock opens the days for that alarm instead of making a copy.
:::

## Change or pause a single alarm schedule

Go to "Alarm" > "Set alarm" and select the alarm schedule you want to change.

**Alarm on / Alarm off** Pauses this alarm schedule only. Your other alarms still go off.

**Set time** Change the time of the alarm.

**Set days** Change the days the alarm goes off.

## Delete an alarm

Select the alarm, choose "Delete" and confirm with "Delete alarm".

<div class="hb-screens">
  <figure><img src="/screens/custom-alarms/delete-confirm.png" alt="Delete alarm confirmation"><figcaption>Confirm with "Delete alarm".</figcaption></figure>
</div>

> [!NOTE]
> **Always one alarm** You can't delete your last alarm. If you don't want it to go off, choose "Alarm off" instead.

## Quick toggle alarms

Double press the stop button to see your coming alarms. Turn the selector wheel to an alarm and press to turn it off or on for its coming day. The change is saved to that alarm’s schedule.

## Good to know

**Sound and wake-up light** The alarm sound, volume and wake-up light are the same for all your alarms. Set them in "Alarm" > "Select sound" and "Alarm" > "Settings" > "Wake-up light".

**Turn all alarms off** "All alarms on" at the top of the Alarm menu turns all alarms off and on in one go.

**Clock face** If you have multiple alarms coming up, multiple dots appear to show the number of alarms.

**Going back** Switching to "Weekdays only" or "Repeat daily" keeps your custom alarms saved, so they are ready when you switch back.
